NextMove privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

NextMove is a free chess-teaching app for the whole family, created by Dr. Roni Gershonovitch. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. NextMove does not ask for, collect, store on a server or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your games, lesson stars, puzzle results, saved mistakes and settings are kept only in your browser's storage on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data in your browser, or uninstalling the app, deletes them.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered by our hosting provider, Vercel. Like any web server, it briefly processes the technical information needed to send a file to your device, such as your IP address, and may keep it in short-lived server logs for security and reliability. NextMove does not use these logs to identify or track anyone. Once the app is installed it works offline and makes no requests at all.

Voices

Elias, the teacher, speaks in a voice that was generated in advance with OpenAI text-to-speech and is shipped inside the app; NextMove never contacts OpenAI. When a sentence has no recording, your device's own voice reads it, and NextMove prefers voices that work on the device. If you choose an online voice yourself, or your browser offers no other, the text being read (lesson and coach text, never your games or progress) is sent to your browser's maker to be spoken.

Children

NextMove is made for children as well as adults. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. It contains no links that leave the app, no chat and no way to contact other people.

Third parties

No analytics, advertising or social-media services are built into NextMove. Credits for the open-source parts (the Stockfish chess engine, the chess pieces, the Lichess puzzles, fonts) are listed in the app under Settings › Licences and source code.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
